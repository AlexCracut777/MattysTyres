Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mattys Tyres collects when you visit this website, contact us or book our mobile tyre fitting service, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. We handle personal information in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988 to 2018.

In short: this website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We only collect what you choose to send us, such as your name, phone number, location and tyre size, and we use it to quote for and carry out your job. We never sell your information.

1. Who we are

Mattys Tyres is a mobile tyre fitting business serving Dublin and Leinster, Ireland. For this policy, Mattys Tyres is the "data controller" for the personal information described here.

You can contact us about anything in this policy by phone or WhatsApp on 083 445 3800.

2. What information we collect

Information you give us when you phone, message or use the quote form:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The location or area where you need us, which may be your home, workplace or a roadside location
  • Your tyre size, the service you need and the type of customer you are (for example private motorist or fleet)
  • Anything else you choose to tell us, such as your vehicle details or photos of your tyre
  • For business and fleet customers: your company name and contact details, and the details of the vehicles we service

Information created when we do a job: the work carried out, the parts supplied, the price and payment records, and any invoices or receipts.

Please don't send us sensitive information we don't need, such as bank details by message.

3. How we use your information, and our legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry, give you a quote and carry out the job. Our legal basis is that it is needed to take steps at your request before a contract and to perform that contract.
  • To find you and reach you on the day, including calling you when we are on the way. Same legal basis as above.
  • To keep accounting and tax records. Our legal basis is our legal obligation under Irish tax law.
  • To manage ongoing service for fleet and business customers, such as scheduled tyre checks. Our legal basis is performance of our contract with that business, and our legitimate interest in running the account.
  • To deal with complaints, warranty claims or legal disputes. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in protecting our business.

We do not use your information for marketing unless you have asked us to keep in touch, and you can tell us to stop at any time.

4. The quote form and WhatsApp

The quote form on this website does not store or send your details anywhere by itself. When you press "Send my request", your browser opens WhatsApp with a message already filled in, addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

Once you send it, the message is delivered through WhatsApp, which is provided by WhatsApp Ireland Limited (part of Meta). WhatsApp's handling of your information is covered by its own privacy policy, which you can read at whatsapp.com/legal. If you would rather not use WhatsApp, please phone us instead.

5. Who we share your information with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We only share it where needed:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as our phone and messaging services, payment providers and accountant
  • Your employer or fleet operator, if we are working on a company vehicle you drive
  • Authorities such as Revenue or An Garda Síochána, only where the law requires it

Some of these providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on legal safeguards approved under GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

6. This website, cookies and hosting

  • No cookies or trackers. This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking tools.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Links. Phone and WhatsApp links open apps on your device. Links to other websites are covered by those websites' own policies.

7. How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a job: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Job, invoice and payment records: 6 years, as required for Irish tax purposes.
  • Fleet and business account details: for as long as we work together, then as above.

Messages in WhatsApp and our phone's call history are cleared in line with these periods.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we no longer need it or are not required to keep it
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to us using it based on our legitimate interests
  • Ask for your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, contact us on 083 445 3800. We will reply within one month. We may need to confirm who you are first.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ireland's data protection authority, at dataprotection.ie. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

9. Keeping your information safe

We keep the information we hold to a minimum and protect it with sensible measures, such as password-protected and encrypted devices, and secure messaging. This website is served over a secure HTTPS connection.

10. Changes to this policy and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.

Questions about this policy or your information? Call or WhatsApp us on 083 445 3800.
